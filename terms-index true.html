3. What TrueIQ is, and what it is not

TrueIQ is an online cognitive assessment built for education and entertainment. That framing matters, so we want to be direct about it.

3.1 Not a clinical assessment

Our test is not a clinical or professional IQ evaluation. It is not administered or interpreted by a licensed psychologist, it is not equivalent to instruments such as the WAIS or Stanford–Binet, and it has not been independently validated or normed against a standardised population.

Do not use your score for academic placement, employment decisions, medical or psychological purposes, immigration, or membership applications. If you need a clinical assessment, see a licensed professional.

3.2 What can affect your result

Scores move around for reasons that have nothing to do with ability — tiredness, stress, distraction, time of day, screen size, connection speed, and how familiar you are with this style of question. Treat your result as a snapshot taken under particular conditions, not a fixed measure of who you are.

6. Subscriptions, billing, and automatic renewal

6.1 What you are agreeing to pay

When you subscribe, you are charged $1.00 immediately for seven days of access. On day 8, unless you have cancelled, your subscription starts at $29.99 per month and renews automatically each month until you cancel.

By entering your payment details and completing checkout, you authorise us to charge that card or account on this schedule. We show these terms next to the payment button before you pay, and we send them again in your receipt.

Example. You sign up on 1 March. You pay $1.00 that day and have access through 7 March. On 8 March you are charged $29.99, and again on 8 April, 8 May, and so on, until you cancel.

6.2 Currency and taxes

Prices are in US dollars. Where sales tax or VAT applies, it is added at checkout and shown before you pay. If your card is issued outside the United States, your bank may add a conversion or foreign transaction fee. That fee is set by your bank, not by us, and we cannot refund it.

6.3 Price changes

We may change subscription pricing. If your rate is going to change, we will email you at least 30 days beforehand, and the new rate will only apply from a renewal date after that notice. You can cancel before then and pay nothing further.

6.4 Payment problems

If a charge fails, we will retry it over the following days and email you. If it has not gone through after 30 days, we will suspend access until the balance is settled. Any fee your bank charges for a declined payment is between you and your bank.

6.5 Payment processing

Payments are handled by Stripe and PayPal. We never receive your full card number. Your use of those services is also subject to their own terms.

6.6 Renewal reminders

We will email you before your trial converts to a paid subscription. That reminder goes out at least three days before the first $29.99 charge, and it states the amount, the date it will be taken, and a direct link to cancel.

We will also email you before any charge that follows a price increase, at least 30 days in advance, as set out in Section 6.3.

These notices go to the address on your account, which is why Section 5 asks you to keep it current.

If you think a charge is wrong, email support@trueiqreport.com first. We can usually resolve billing problems within a day or two, which is faster than a bank dispute.

This does not limit any right you have to dispute a genuinely unauthorised charge.

7. Cancelling

Cancel whenever you like, for any reason, through the Cancellation Portal. It takes a few clicks and requires no phone call, no chat, and no explanation. If you would rather email, write to support@trueiqreport.com and we will process it.

Cancelling stops all future charges. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for, then ends. We do not prorate or refund the unused part of a period — see Section 8.

To avoid the $29.99 charge, cancel before your 7-day trial ends. Cancelling on day 8 or later means that month has already been charged.

Cancelling a subscription is not the same as deleting your account. See Section 17 if you want your data removed as well.

Billing errors are a separate matter and are always put right in full: a charge taken twice for the same period, a charge made after you properly cancelled, an incorrect amount, a payment the cardholder did not authorise, or a service we discontinue after you have paid for it.
