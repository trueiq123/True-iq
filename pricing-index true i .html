How do I cancel?

Go to the Cancellation Portal, enter your account email, and follow the steps. There is no phone call and no retention pitch.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

Subscription charges are nonrefundable. If you write to us we will review your situation and make a determination, but a review is not a promise of a refund.

Billing errors are always put right in full — duplicate charges, charges after a valid cancellation, incorrect amounts, or payments you did not authorise. See the Refund Policy.

The surest way to avoid a charge you do not want is to cancel before your trial ends.

How long does the test take?

Around 20 minutes. You can move between questions and change answers before you submit.

For a result worth having, give yourself uninterrupted time rather than squeezing it in.

Is this a real IQ test?

It is a genuine assessment, but it is built for education and entertainment. It is not a clinical evaluation, it is not administered by a psychologist, and it is not equivalent to instruments like the WAIS or Stanford–Binet.

Do not use your score for job applications, academic placement, or medical decisions. For that you need a licensed professional.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The test runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or computer, with nothing to install.
